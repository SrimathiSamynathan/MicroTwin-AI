Plant growth records: stem and root analysis

Three records of the same radish tray. Each one measures stem and root length, and the twin uses their balance to judge growth quality. These are sample values until you enter your measurements.

StemRoot

How the analysis works

  1. Photograph. Take a side-on photo of the tray with a ruler or a 5 cm marker in the frame.
  2. Sample. Pick about 10 seedlings per record, on days 3, 6 and 9.
  3. Measure. Stem length runs from the seed base to the top of the stem. Root length runs from the seed base to the root tip. analyze_growth.py does this from the photo.
  4. Compute. Average the sample, then calculate the root to stem ratio and stem growth per day.
  5. Feed the twin. The records become training data and calibrate the growth model after every harvest.
